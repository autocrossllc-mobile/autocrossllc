AutoCross LLC Shop Policies

Clear, transparent guidelines ensuring quality, safety, and reliable service timelines.

Diagnostic Scans & Inspection Fees

Mobile computer diagnostics and complete hands-on vehicle inspections require an upfront diagnostic fee (typically $100–$150 depending on location). Diagnostic fees are applied directly toward your repair balance when service is authorized.

Deposit Terms & Payment Milestones

A 50% deposit on labor is required before work begins on major mechanical jobs, engine overhauls, or transmission swaps. For custom LS/LT performance builds ranging from $8,000 to $70,000+, custom parts, engine blocks, and crate components must be paid 100% upfront.

Customer-Supplied Parts Policy

While we install customer-supplied performance components upon request, AutoCross LLC cannot offer warranties on parts provided directly by clients. Labor warranties apply exclusively to components sourced directly through AutoCross LLC suppliers.

Vehicle Completion & Storage Fees

Clients are notified immediately upon job completion. Vehicles or equipment left on shop premises beyond 48 hours without prior arrangement will incur a storage fee of $50/day. Unclaimed items after 30 days are subject to standard California mechanic's liens.

Warranty Coverage

All general auto maintenance and OEM engine replacements backed by AutoCross LLC include a 12-month / 12,000-mile limited labor warranty. Custom high-horsepower LS/LT engine builds and racing configurations carry spec-specific warranties outlined on final contracts.

Towing & Roadside Dispatch

Mobile roadside assistance and transport dispatch services must be authorized prior to technician deployment. Roadside dispatch service fees are non-refundable once service vehicles are en route.
