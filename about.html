Built From The Street Up

Family Legacy, Professional Craftsmanship, And Uncompromising LS Performance

Our Roots

A Family Tradition Of Building Cars

Car building isn't just a business for us; it’s a generational craft. My father built cars, teaching me early on that every bolt turned is a test of character and precision. Today, seeing my kids roll up their sleeves and dive into wrenching alongside me keeps that torch burning brighter than ever.

At AutoCross LLC, family commitment, safety, and a life well-built form the bedrock of everything we do. We build lasting relationships with our clients because when we hand you the keys, we treat your vehicle with the same obsessive care and professionalism we demand for our own families.

The Journey

From Street Repairs To Mobile Mastery

Long before AutoCross LLC had a dedicated shop or our advanced mobile setup, it started right out on my mom’s street. Working under streetlights, swapping components in the open air, and diagnosing stubborn electrical gremlins taught me resourcefulness. There were no shortcuts—just pure grit and determination.

That street-level foundation evolved into having our own physical shop space, which eventually paved the way for our specialized mobile service model. That growth allows us to bring high-level diagnostics and custom performance directly to you across Southern California.

Our core philosophy remains unchanged: Built Not Rushed.

Our Obsession

Chevrolet, LS Builds & Forced Induction

We live and breathe Chevrolet performance. From classic muscle to modern powerhouses, we have a deep passion for Chevrolet Camaros, Silverados, Monte Carlos, and El Caminos. Whether it's dialing in a clean 3rd Gen Camaro, a sleek 4th Gen, or a dominating 5th Gen, we know these platforms inside and out.

When it comes to powerplants, we specialize in legendary LS builds—especially bulletproof 5.3L cast iron LM7 engines equipped with turbochargers or ProChargers for maximum street and track dominance.

Expertise & Capabilities

What AutoCross LLC Is Known For

AutoCross LLC has built its reputation on engineering excellence across complex drivetrain configurations. We are widely recognized for custom AWD LS swaps, FWD LS swaps, and RWD LS swaps that push the limits of packaging and reliability.

Beyond high-horsepower builds, we provide complete, professional automotive care:

  • Regular Tunes & Tune-Ups: Keeping your daily driver or weekend cruiser running crisp, efficient, and dependable.
  • Performance Calibration: Custom dyno and street tuning tailored for maximum horsepower, drivability, and safety.
  • General Mechanical Repairs: Comprehensive diagnostics, suspension, electrical, and drivetrain service.
  • Bodywork & Custom Modifications: Tailored fabrication and styling to ensure your vehicle looks as aggressive as it performs.
