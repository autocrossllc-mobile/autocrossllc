BUILT FROM THE STREET UP

The Journey, The Passion, and The Grit Behind AutoCross LLC

Our Story

We didn't start in a sterile corporate showroom with endless capital. We started where real car culture is born: on the asphalt, with basic tools, late nights, and an uncompromising drive to figure out how machines work.

Chapter 1: The Foundation

Wrenching on the Street

Long before AutoCross LLC had a name or a bay, it started right outside on the street at my mom’s house. Working under streetlights, swapping components in the open air, and diagnosing stubborn electrical gremlins taught me resourcefulness. There were no shortcuts—just pure determination to get the job done right.

That grit forged our core motto: Built Not Rushed.

Chapter 2: The Pro Shops

Mastering the Craft

Taking that street-level hustle into professional mechanic shops elevated everything. Working day-in and day-out alongside seasoned technicians, mastering advanced diagnostics, and tackling complex modern powertrain architectures refined our technical precision. We learned the ins and outs of what makes factory systems fail—and how to build them bulletproof.

Chapter 3: Today

AutoCross LLC — Mobile & Performance

Today, AutoCross LLC brings that lifetime of hands-on experience directly to you. Operating as a family-owned and operated mobile shop, we bridge the gap between high-end shop expertise and ultimate convenience. Whether we're bringing a daily driver back to life or unleashing reliable horsepower, our roots stay grounded in honest work and mechanical integrity.

What Drives Us

Rooted in family values, backed by deep automotive passion.

LS Swap Specialists

As true Chevy enthusiasts, GM LS powerplants are in our blood. From clean engine integration and custom wiring harnesses to valvetrain upgrades and reliable performance tunes, we specialize in making LS power function flawlessly.

All Makes & Models

While our heartbeat is high-performance GM engineering, our mobile diagnostics and service capabilities cover all makes and models. From complex electrical troubleshooting to routine maintenance, we service it all.

Family Owned & Operated

We treat every vehicle like it belongs to our own family. Honesty, transparency, and top-tier workmanship are built into every single job we take on—no corporate runaround, just pure dedication.
