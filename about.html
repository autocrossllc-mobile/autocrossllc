Our Story

We didn't start in a sterile corporate showroom with endless capital. We started where real car culture is born: on the asphalt, with basic tools, late nights, and an uncompromising drive to figure out how machines work.

Chapter 1: The Foundation

Wrenching On The Street

Long before AutoCross LLC had a name or a bay, it started right outside on the street at my mom’s house. Working under streetlights, swapping components in the open air, and diagnosing stubborn electrical gremlins taught me resourcefulness. There were no shortcuts—just pure determination to get the job done right.

That grit forged our core motto: Built Not Rushed.
